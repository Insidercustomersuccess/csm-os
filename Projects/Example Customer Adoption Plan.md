# Example Customer Adoption Plan

> [!example] Fictional example
> This fictional Project demonstrates how a finite customer outcome connects to an Organisation, stakeholder and Meeting.

## Outcome

[[Example Customer]] has launched one personalisation use case and can assess it against a customer-approved baseline and target.

## Current position

The use case has been selected, but ownership, baseline and target still need confirmation.

## Success measures

- Customer sponsor confirmed.
- Implementation owner confirmed.
- Baseline recorded.
- Target approved by the customer.
- Use case launched and reviewed.

## Risks and dependencies

- Measurement may be defined after implementation begins.
- Required customer data may not be available in time.
- Ownership may remain distributed across several teams.

## Key decisions

- Start with one use case rather than a broad programme.
- Do not claim progress as successful until the agreed customer measure is available.
