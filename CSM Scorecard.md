# CSM Scorecard

> [!important] Canonical boundary
> The Google Sheet is the canonical structured scorecard. This note records its purpose, route and operating rules. Do not copy all scorecard values into Markdown.

## Live Google Sheet

- **Status:** Setup required
- **Spreadsheet:** Add the verified Google Sheet link here
- **Workspace/account:** Record after verification
- **Owner:** UK Customer Success
- **Cadence:** Weekly, with quarterly rollover
- **Last verified:** Not yet verified

## Required tabs

1. `Dashboard`
2. `Accounts`
3. `OKRs`
4. `KPIs`
5. `Advocacy & Marketing`
6. `Definitions & Sources`

## Layer definitions

- **Accounts:** tier-first structured portfolio status and next outcomes.
- **OKRs:** time-bounded changes the team intends to achieve this quarter.
- **KPIs:** ongoing operational or commercial health measures.
- **Advocacy & Marketing:** customer contributions CS owns or materially influences.
- **Definitions & Sources:** exact metric definition, numerator, denominator, eligibility, owner, cadence and authoritative source.

## Operating rules

- Every reported percentage needs a traceable numerator, denominator and target.
- `Done` requires evidence.
- `N/A` means explicitly ineligible; blank means unassessed.
- Missing or stale evidence means `Not assessable`, not automatically Red.
- A metric appears in one layer only. Link rather than duplicate it.
- The Sheet summarises official sources; it does not silently replace CRM or another declared authority.
- Confirm before any external write or sharing change.

## Weekly leadership view

The Dashboard should show:

1. portfolio status and source health;
2. regional OKRs needing intervention;
3. KPI and threshold exposure;
4. customer advocacy and marketing gaps;
5. no more than five tier-first account exceptions;
6. no more than five leadership decisions or interventions.

## Quarter rollover

At quarter end, preserve the final Sheet or protected quarter tabs, record the outcome and evidence, verify the next quarter's definitions and sources, then update this note's link and verification date if the live spreadsheet changes.
